azad: questions and answers

Your data

Where is my data?

On your phone, only. There's no account and no server. We can't see it, and nobody else can.

What happens if I lose my phone?

Turn on backup (Settings → Backup). It saves a copy to your iCloud Drive or a folder you pick, by itself. On a new phone, install azad and tap "Restore" on the welcome screen.

Should I set a backup password?

If your backup sits somewhere others might open, yes. It's encrypted on your phone before it's saved. Keep the password safe: without it, nobody can open the backup, including us.

Can I move my history from another app?

Yes: Strong, Hevy and MyFitnessPal. Export a CSV from that app, then Settings → Your data → Import.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app. Backups you made stay where you saved them; delete those files yourself if you want them gone.

Does anything ever leave my phone?

Only what you turn on: barcode lookups (the barcode number), backup (to your own iCloud or folder), crash reports (if you said yes; never your data), an AI you connect (only what you send), and the store, if you buy Pro. Settings → Privacy lists each one.

Training

How does the app pick my weights?

When you hit the top of the rep range at a steady effort, the weight goes up next time. If not, it stays. Tap ⓘ on any exercise to see why.

What's a block, and what's the lighter week?

A few weeks of building, then a lighter week so tiredness can clear. The next block starts from what you earned.

Can I change my block?

Plan → Change this block. Make it longer or shorter, take the lighter week next, or start the next block today. Your history never changes.

Something hurts.

Mark pain when you log a set. The weight won't go up on that lift until you say it feels fine. If pain is sharp or lasts, see a doctor or physio. azad isn't medical advice.

Can I swap an exercise?

Yes: on Plan, tap the exercise, then swap it.

I missed a workout.

That's fine. Skip it on Today, or just do the next one when you can. Nothing is marked as missed.

Food

Does food logging work offline?

Yes, the food list is on your phone. Scanning a new barcode needs the internet, and only if you allowed lookups.

AI

Do I need an AI?

No. Everything works without one.

What does the free AI do?

On supported phones, your phone's own AI (Apple Intelligence, Gemini Nano) turns a sentence like "bench 3×8 at 60" into a log. You check it before it's saved.

What does connecting my own AI cost?

Pro, plus your own account with that company (Anthropic or Meta). They charge you for what you use; set a spend limit in their console. azad shows the cost and what's sent before you connect.

Pro

What's free and what's Pro?

Free for good: logging, programs, progression, food, import, backup and your phone's own AI. Pro adds connecting your own AI, the tally plan, and future planning and stats tools.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once, on the App Store or Google Play.

I bought Pro on another phone.

Settings → Pro → Restore purchase, signed in to the same Apple or Google account. Pro moves within the same store only (iPhone to iPhone, Android to Android).

Refunds?

The store handles refunds: reportaproblem.apple.com for iPhone, or Google Play's order history.

Still stuck?

Ask on GitHub Issues (it needs a free GitHub account). Please don't post your workouts, food or anything personal there: issues are public.
