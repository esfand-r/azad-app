Azad privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026.

In short: everything you log stays on your phone. There's no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We (the developer) don't receive your workouts, food, body measurements, tally or anything you type.

What stays on your phone

Workouts, programs, food, body measurements, your tally and its notes, settings, and any AI text. Deleting the app deletes them.

What can leave your phone, and only when you choose

  • Barcode lookups: when you scan a product not on your phone, only its barcode number goes to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org). Off until you allow it.
  • Your phone's AI: on iPhone, Apple Intelligence runs on the phone and nothing is sent. On Android, Gemini Nano runs on the phone, but Google's ML Kit sends Google usage statistics (device model, app version, request sizes and times, an identifier), not what you type. Off until you turn it on.
  • An AI you connect (Pro): only what you send, only when you tap, from your phone to the company you connected (Anthropic or Meta) using your own key. When you ask a question, the app lists the parts of your data that go with it (for example your last training blocks, or two weeks of food totals) before you send, and you can leave any part out. Your tally is never sent. The key stays in your phone's secure storage. That company's privacy terms apply.
  • Your conversations with your AI are kept on your phone (and in your backup), and you can delete them.
  • Backup: if you turn it on, a file in your iCloud Drive or a folder you pick. With a password, it is encrypted on your phone first (AES-256).
  • Crash reports: only if you say yes. When the app breaks, a short report goes to Sentry (EU, Germany) with what broke, the app version and the phone model. Never your data; no user id.
  • Buying Pro: handled by the App Store or Google Play with your Apple or Google account; their terms apply. The app only asks the store whether Pro was bought. We get no payment details.
  • Links: how-to videos and help links open in your browser.

This website

These pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. They use no cookies, scripts or analytics; GitHub keeps standard server logs (such as IP addresses) under its own privacy statement.

Children

Azad is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Your data is on your phone, under your control.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date, and the app's privacy page will match.
